Why MBBS Abroad?

MBBS abroad is an attractive option for Indian students for various reasons.

Recognition in India

The National Medical Commission (NMC) of India recognizes medical degrees earned from international universities, allowing foreign MBBS graduates to practice in India after passing the NEXT exam.

Global Learning and Knowledge

Studying MBBS abroad provides exposure to a global medical environment, helping students understand diverse healthcare systems and diagnoses.

Globally Competitive Infrastructure

Medical colleges abroad offer world-class facilities and modern infrastructure that meet global medical standards.

Acceptance of NEET

NEET scores are accepted for MBBS abroad, with only a qualifying score required instead of extremely high marks.

Cost of Education

Medical education abroad is often more affordable compared to India, including tuition fees and living costs.

Anti-Ragging Campus

Many foreign medical colleges enforce strict anti-ragging policies to ensure a safe learning environment.

Admission Process

  • Choose a Country & University – Research and select your preferred university.
  • Submit Application & Documents – Passport, 12th marks, NEET score (if required).
  • Receive Offer Letter – University sends confirmation.
  • Visa & Travel Arrangements – Get student visa approval.
  • Start Your MBBS Journey! – Fly to your dream destination.
  • On-Arrival Assistance – Guidance for accommodation and university onboarding.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) in 12th grade.
  • NEET qualification (required for some countries).
  • Valid passport & student visa.
  • Age requirement: Minimum 17 years at the time of admission.
  • English proficiency as required by the university.
  • Medical fitness certificate as per university guidelines.
